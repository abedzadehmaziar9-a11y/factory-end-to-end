# The Factory, End to End

**A collected technical library on how a modern factory works**

Compiled & researched by **Maziar Abedzadeh**

---

## About

A 272-page collected reference covering the software, hardware, and planning systems that run a modern manufacturing plant — from the sensor and the bolt on the floor, through controllers, networks, motors and pneumatics, up to production planning and the data journey to the cloud.

## Contents

**Part One — The Integration Layer** (Issues 01–19)
Manufacturing operating systems · the Siemens stack · floor protocols · Rockwell & CIP · Beckhoff and EtherCAT · motors and drives · logistics and inventory · sensors · conveyors · Atlas Copco torque systems · Festo pneumatics · Emerson · material handling · production planning (MRP & Heijunka) · factory software stacks · KPIs and OEE · demand forecasting · Excel for planning · appendix and device index

**Part Two — The Factory Floor** Bolted-joint engineering · fastener types and standards · the fastener catalog · MPS, MRP & MRP II

**Part Three — Industry 4.0** The factory cloud (plain-English edition) · from sensor to cloud (technical deep-dive)

## Sources & scope

The material collected here is drawn entirely from publicly available, publishable sources. It is general reference material: it was not compiled for, and does not describe, any specific factory, plant, or company. Company and product names used as examples are illustrative only.

## Other work

### Differential Gene Expression Analysis of Glioblastoma Microstructures

An R-based computational transcriptomics study using the Ivy Glioblastoma Atlas Project
(Ivy GAP) dataset — comparing gene expression between the Infiltrating Tumor (IT) edge
and the Cellular Tumor (CT) core across 122 RNA-seq samples from 10 tumors.

Built with **DESeq2** (differential expression) and **WGCNA** (co-expression network
analysis) in R. Full write-up, figures, and analysis scripts:
[`glioblastoma-dge/`](glioblastoma-dge/)
